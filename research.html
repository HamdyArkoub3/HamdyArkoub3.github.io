Research

My research focuses on computational nuclear materials, molten salt corrosion, alloy degradation, and multiscale modeling for advanced nuclear energy systems.

Research Themes

Molten Salt Corrosion

I study corrosion mechanisms in Ni-based alloys exposed to molten fluoride salts using reactive molecular dynamics and first-principles calculations.

Reactive Interfaces

My work examines fluorine adsorption, chromium dissolution, surface orientation effects, and interfacial reaction pathways in extreme environments.

Multiscale Modeling

I connect DFT, ReaxFF molecular dynamics, kinetic Monte Carlo, and future engineering-scale models to predict materials degradation over longer time and length scales.
