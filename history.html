History of Egypt

I am deeply interested in the history of Egypt, from the earliest formation of the Egyptian state to the pharaonic, Greco-Roman, Coptic, Islamic, and modern periods.

I approach history as another way to study complex systems: civilizations shaped by geography, climate, technology, belief, governance, conflict, memory, and resilience across long time scales.
